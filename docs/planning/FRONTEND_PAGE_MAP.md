# FRONTEND_PAGE_MAP.md

The inventory below lists the currently implemented routes first. As of `TASK-0141`, EPIC-122's canonical workpage route posture under `/runs/:workflowRunId/workpages/*` is active and primary-discoverable in the frontend; the older `/demo/logistics/workpages/*` routes remain as compatibility aliases and secondary demo access paths.

As of `TASK-0142`, EPIC-123 also freezes the next schedule artifact-backed posture around the Stage04 `planning.draft_weekly_schedule.workbook` family and reserves `/runs/:workflowRunId/workpages/schedule-v0/artifacts/:artifactVersionId` as the canonical future artifact route. That schedule artifact route is planned, not implemented yet.
