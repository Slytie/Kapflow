# Ops One-Pager - Stage 4 workflows

## What Ops gets
A single place to operate workflows with:
- immutable spreadsheet / document versions
- explicit approvals and ownership
- timeline-based auditability
- stable publication plus auditable deltas where the domain demands it

## Payroll commitments
- officialness is defined by promotion pointers
- drift is recorded and visible
- lock and finalize remain explicit
- no raw bank account numbers in artifacts

## Schedule Planning commitments (contract commitments in Stage 4)
Stage 4 includes Schedule Planning to validate semantics and auditability under live-day deltas, but does not require shipping the runtime automation path yet.

## Schedule Planning commitments
- Stage06 publishes a stable base schedule
- Stage07 handles inevitable disruptions through explicit replan deltas
- supervisor and operations-manager review paths are explicit
- no-show, vehicle, delay, and coverage risks remain attributable

## Important repo rule
Runbook packs, approval packets, and generated spreadsheets may help operators, but they do not replace the authoritative workflow pack and timeline.
